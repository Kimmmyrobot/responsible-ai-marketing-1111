Marketing workflow playbook

A Responsible AI Workflow for Marketing Teams

Marketing teams can gain useful capacity from AI without turning every asset into unverified output. The key is to assign the system a bounded role inside a visible editorial process.

Start here

How to use this resource

The pressure to publish quickly makes marketing an obvious place to experiment. It also creates risk: invented claims, generic language, accidental disclosure, inconsistent voice, and assets that no one can trace back to an approved source.

The Coffee Cup AI workflow design services helps teams redesign workflows around human ownership. This playbook treats AI as a research and drafting aid while keeping strategy, factual approval, brand judgment, and publication authority with people.

Play 1
01

Start with an approved brief

The human owner defines the audience, objective, offer, evidence, channel, constraints, and call to action. Approved claims and source links are attached before drafting begins.

A complete brief reduces the temptation to let the system invent positioning or fill evidence gaps. It also makes review faster because the reviewer knows what the asset is supposed to accomplish.

  • Audience, objective, and channel
  • Approved claims and supporting sources
  • Brand, legal, and format constraints
Play 2
02

Separate research from claims

AI can help organize questions, themes, and source material, but a person verifies every material fact and records the authoritative source. Generated summaries are treated as leads, not evidence.

Create a claim ledger for statistics, testimonials, comparisons, and product statements. The ledger travels with the asset through approval.

  • Claim and exact wording
  • Authoritative source and access date
  • Verifier and approval status
Play 3
03

Draft in controlled stages

Use the system for bounded stages such as outlines, variants, or restructuring. Keep the approved brief and evidence visible, and require the writer to resolve unsupported language before the asset enters review.

A staged process preserves authorship. The human editor can judge voice, empathy, context, and whether the message serves the audience rather than merely sounding fluent.

  • Outline review
  • Evidence-bound draft
  • Human edit for voice and usefulness
Play 4
04

Publish with a learning record

Before publication, record approvals, source checks, and any meaningful AI assistance according to the organization's policy. Afterward, compare results with the original objective rather than counting output volume.

The learning record helps the team identify which uses save time without eroding quality and which steps require stronger guidance.

  • Final factual and brand approval
  • Publication and version record
  • Outcome measure and retrospective

Questions teams ask

Questions and answers

Can AI write a complete marketing asset?

A system can produce a draft, but accountable people should own strategy, sources, claims, voice, review, and the publication decision.

What belongs in a claim ledger?

Include statistics, comparisons, testimonials, guarantees, product capabilities, and other statements that require evidence or approval.

How should success be measured?

Measure the business and audience outcome alongside quality, correction, review time, and risk signals—not just the number of assets produced.

Operating takeaway

Responsible marketing use increases capacity by strengthening the workflow around evidence and judgment, not by removing the people responsible for them.
